# Sound credits

The hit, bounce and squeak sounds in this folder are cut from real recordings on
[Freesound](https://freesound.org) (their public previews), trimmed, filtered and
split into takes. AudioDirector plays `<name>_1 .. _8` in place of its synthesised
sounds; anything not here (tin, nick, frame, body, the referee, the crowd) is still
synthesised.
